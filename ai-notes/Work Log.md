- Added `api.maniskill_tasks.solve_stack_three()`: a motion-planned scripted solution for `StackThree-v1` (grasp+place cubeB on cubeC, then cubeA on cubeB; privileged-state, not vision — same "state-in, trajectory-out" pattern as the OpenAI GPT-6 Astra robotics write-ups), adapted from mani_skill's own `StackCube-v1` solution (`examples/motionplanning/panda/solutions/stack_cube.py`) and factored into a reusable `_pick_and_stack` helper since it's needed twice.
